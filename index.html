Hackenbush Workbench

Draw a Blue-Red Hackenbush position on the ground. Every such position is equal to a dyadic rational. Each grounded piece gets a dot and its value on the board, and the panel adds them up.

A stalk by the simplicity rule

Pick a stalk from the board or type one, ground edge first (B = blue, R = red). Cutting edge j of a stalk removes everything above it and leaves the first j − 1 edges. So row k's options are values already found in the rows above, and its value is the simplest number strictly between Left's best option and Right's best option. Left is whichever color counts as positive. Best options are underlined.
